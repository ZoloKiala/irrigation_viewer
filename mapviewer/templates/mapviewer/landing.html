How it works

From layer to insight in three steps

  1. 1

    Pick a country and layer

    Open the map, select Zimbabwe or South Africa, and toggle a layer in the sidebar.

  2. 2

    Define an area of interest

    Draw a polygon with the pen tool, or click an admin boundary — the popup gives you a "Run analysis" button.

  3. 3

    Read the breakdown

    Area-by-suitability-class chart appears in the sidebar; tweak the layer's palette or opacity in the Tweaks panel.
